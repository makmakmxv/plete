About PLETE

PLETE is an offline tricycle fare guide for Tuguegarao City. Choose where you are going and see the fare, so commuters know what to pay.

Fare data

Fares are based on the rates published by the LGU of Tuguegarao City. PLETE is an independent project and is not an official LGU app. For official information, contact the LGU.

Privacy

  • PLETE has no accounts and does not ask for personal information.
  • It does not use your location.
  • The app saves its own files on your phone so it can work offline. These stay on your device.
  • The Call button only opens your phone's dialer.
  • The website host may keep standard server records when the page is first loaded, which PLETE does not control.

Terms of use

  • PLETE is for information only. Fares may change, so confirm with the LGU if you are unsure.
  • The app is provided as is, without any guarantee that every fare shown is current or error-free.
  • Report overcharging to the LGU hotline shown in the app.
